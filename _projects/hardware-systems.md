These projects come from coursework and lab work at Sharif. They are the hardware counterpart to the machine-learning work on the rest of this site, and the reason I still care about what happens after a model is trained: clocks, memory, and the device it actually has to run on.

## FPGA and digital design

- [**CORDIC on Zynq FPGA**](https://github.com/mohammadKazzazi/Cordic_Algorithm_With_ZynQ_FPGA) — Verilog implementation in Vivado.
- [**FFT on Zynq FPGA**](https://github.com/mohammadKazzazi/FFT_using_ZynQ_FPGA) — with VGA output.
- [**Multi-cycle MIPS processor**](https://github.com/mohammadKazzazi/Multi-cycle-MIPS-implementation) — Verilog.
- ASIC/FPGA system design and VLSI coursework.

## GPU computing

- [**Parallel merge sort**](https://github.com/mohammadKazzazi/Merge_Sort_CUDA) on GPU with CUDA.
- [**Heat-transfer simulation**](https://github.com/mohammadKazzazi/Heat_Transformation_with_Texture_Memory_CUDA) using CUDA texture memory.

## Communications and embedded systems

- [**Digital telecommunication system simulation**](https://github.com/mohammadKazzazi/simulate-a-digital-telecommunication-system).
- **Smart IoT socket** firmware on Arduino and ESP32/ESP8266 for the Artificial Creature Laboratory, after evaluating IoT platforms and network protocols for the lab's AI platform.

## Tools

Vivado, Xilinx ISE, ModelSim, LTspice, Zynq FPGA, Raspberry Pi, Arduino, ESP32/ESP8266, CUDA.
