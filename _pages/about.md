I am an M.Sc. student in [Electrical Engineering at EPFL](https://www.epfl.ch/schools/sti/), Lausanne. My research lies at the intersection of computer vision and multimodal learning. I am particularly interested in vision-language and vision-language-action models, and in making them efficient, reliable, and trustworthy enough to deploy on real devices.

At EPFL, I am a volunteer research assistant at the [VITA Lab](https://www.epfl.ch/labs/vita/), where I work with Yasaman Haghighi on guidance for diffusion and flow models. We study how classifier-free guidance affects approximation error and propose a training-free guidance method that reuses the model's own clean-prediction history, improving generation quality with fewer network evaluations; this work is currently under review at CVPR 2027.

In parallel, I work with [Prof. Siavash Khajavi](https://www.aalto.fi/en/people/siavash-khajavi-haghighat) at [Aalto University](https://www.aalto.fi/en) on knowledge distillation of vision-language models for on-device fire understanding. Our paper, _Distilling Vision-Language Models for On-Device Fire Understanding_, has been accepted at **AAAI 2027 (IAAI)**. I am now investigating prompt optimization and reasoning strategies for vision-language-action models.

I received my B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering (Digital Systems) from [Sharif University of Technology](https://www.sharif.edu/), where I completed my thesis on closed-form TDOA/FDOA localization with [Prof. Rouhollah Amiri](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=sOUZ3cUAAAAJ). During my undergraduate studies, I also worked on medical image analysis at Raderon AI Laboratory with Dr. Soroush Sarabi and on embedded IoT systems with [Prof. Saeed Bagheri Shouraki](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=nR98aV4AAAAJ&hl=en), and served as a teaching assistant for six courses. My background in FPGA and GPU programming continues to shape how I think about deploying models under real-world constraints.
